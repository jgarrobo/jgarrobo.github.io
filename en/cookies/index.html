Cookie policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small files that a website stores in your browser to remember information about your visit. Similar technologies, such as the browser's local storage (localStorage), are subject to the same rules.

Cookies used on this site

This site does not use cookies, first-party or third-party, for analytics, advertising or tracking.

It only stores the following technical preferences in your browser, using local storage and only when you choose them:

NamePurposeDuration
langRemember the language you pick with the ES/EN button.Until you delete it
themeRemember whether you pick light or dark mode.Until you delete it

This data never leaves your browser and is not sent to any server. As it is strictly necessary to provide a service you explicitly request, it is exempt from consent under Article 22.2 of Spanish Law 34/2002 (LSSI).

Third-party services

To work, this site uses:

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which hosts and serves the site. See its privacy statement.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare, Inc.), which serves the icons. See its privacy policy.

Like any web server, these providers may log technical connection data, such as your IP address, in order to serve the files. This site does not ask them to set cookies; any processing they carry out is governed by their own policies.

How to manage or delete this data

You can delete the stored preferences at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. You can also set your browser to block cookies and local storage; the site will still work, but it won't remember your preferences.

Changes to this policy

If the site ever starts using other cookies, this policy will be updated beforehand.
